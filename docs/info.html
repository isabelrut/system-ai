What is the ESPR?

  • gavel ESPR = Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (EU, 2024).
  • eco Creates sustainability requirements for products sold in the EU.
  • recycling Aims to make products more durable, repairable, recyclable, and transparent.
  • schedule Specific DPP requirements will be introduced product group by product group through delegated acts over the coming years.

What is a Digital Product Passport (DPP)?

  • description A digital record containing key product information.
  • qr_code Accessible through a QR code, RFID tag, or similar identifier.
  • hub Follows the product throughout its lifecycle.

Why is the EU introducing DPPs?

  • visibility Improve product transparency.
  • autorenew Support circular economy goals.
  • build_circle Enable repair, reuse, remanufacturing, and recycling.
  • verified Help authorities verify compliance.

What information will a DPP contain?

  • badge Product identification details.
  • inventory_2 Material composition.
  • eco Carbon footprint and environmental data.
  • build Repair and maintenance information.
  • verified Compliance and certification data.
  • recycling End-of-life and recycling instructions.

How does the DPP benefit businesses?

  • gavel Easier compliance with EU regulations.
  • hub Better supply chain visibility.
  • analytics Improved sustainability reporting.
  • autorenew New opportunities for circular business models.

How does the DPP benefit consumers?

  • visibility More transparency about products.
  • compare_arrows Easier comparison of sustainability claims.
  • build_circle Better access to repair and maintenance information.
  • verified_user Increased confidence in product information.

How will this tool help you?

  • fact_check Generate a set of relevant DPP requirements for your organization.
  • gavel Retrieves relevant EU regulations for your situation.
  • rocket_launch Support you in utilizing the DPP to its full potential.

Disclaimer for using the tool:

  • info The DPP regulations are still subject to change. It is the user's responsibility to keep track of these changes.
  • warning This set of requirements has been generated using a Large Language Model. Although it has access to a relevant set of documents about the Digital Product Passport, the used LLM is still sensitive to hallucinations.
  • priority_high Hence, it is recommended to only use these requirements as a starting point in your journey with the DPP. No rights can be derived from this LLM output.
